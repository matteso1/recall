# Complete historical match data — September 28, 2026

Recall now has a resumable collector for paired official Riot Match-v5 match and timeline
responses. This addresses the earlier corpus's missing teammates, enemy inventories, rune pages
and exact purchase events. The collector preserves source data; it does not silently replace the
production models or claim that high-rank purchase frequency proves an optimal decision.

## Sampling and validation

The historical smoke pilot uses 100 existing corpus matches on internal patch 16.18. The fresh
pilot samples 24 current Master/Grandmaster/Challenger ladder players in NA and 24 in EUW, looks
back seven days at ranked solo matches, then filters actual match responses to internal patch
16.19. Histories yielded 391 unique NA seeds and 301 unique EUW seeds. Older-patch games are counted
as excluded before requesting a timeline. Rank provenance is retained privately for the seed
players; other participants' ranks are not inferred from their presence in these matches.

| Sample | Complete pairs | Minute frames | Player frames |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| NA 16.19 | 308 | 8,740 | 87,400 |
| EUW 16.19 | 254 | 6,877 | 68,770 |
| EUW historical 16.18 | 100 | 2,808 | 28,080 |
| Total | 662 | 18,425 | 184,250 |

There are 562 current-patch matches and 3,906 distinct players across the full pilot. All 6,620
player-game records have known roles, two summoner spells and full rune pages. The raw timelines
contain 5,468 undo events and 96,156 skill-up events. NA excluded 83 older-patch matches; EUW
excluded 47. No pairs were unavailable or failed validation. Both regional batches were rerun
with a client that rejects every network request: all 562 completed pairs were reused from cache,
and exclusions were reproduced. These counts describe coverage, not the quality of a new model.

Every completed pair has matching match IDs, matching stable participant identities, ten player
slots, two teams of five, chronological frames containing all ten players, an initial frame and
a final game-end event. Numeric gold, XP, level and CS fields are checked. Raw item purchases,
sales and undo payloads are retained exactly. Full perk pages and skill-up events are available
in the source. Source hashes bind the validation manifest to the two private response files.

The [aggregate coverage report](riot-collection-coverage.json) is generated by
`tools/priors/audit_matches.py`. It verifies those hashes and exports counts only. Raw responses,
seed-player identities, and the development credential remain outside Git and the app build.

## How this changes development

The existing full-planner benchmark remains the production comparison. The fresh data supplies
the inputs needed for the next benchmark generation: full-team context, component transaction
timing, skills, spells and rune pages, with stable identities for split exclusions. Before fitting
new models, reconstruct item transactions against each patch's recipes and final inventory,
including undo, consumption, transformations and quest-slot items. Expose unresolved transactions
as exclusions rather than guessing. Hold out both players and matches before fitting any priors.

Minute snapshots do not supply sub-minute gold/stat observations. Historical enemy positions and
exact enemy gold must not become live features when the app cannot observe them. Kill/objective
events can be aligned only with information available to the live interface at that time.

The current cohort is a bounded pilot, not enough evidence for rare champion-role interactions.
New data has not yet trained production boots, answer weights, bans, runes or builds. The eventual
model needs held-out calibration and role/champion slices; a legality test alone is insufficient.

## Reproduction

See the [collector commands](../../tools/priors/README.md#complete-match-v5-collection). Requests
use a private header credential, conservative per-route spacing and bounded retries honoring
Riot's retry header. Responses are written atomically; rerunning collection reuses and validates
cached pairs. Authentication failure stops with cached progress intact.

Local unit contracts cover resume, filters, identity and frame consistency, truncated timelines,
preserved undo events, credential redaction, rate limiting, routing, cohort provenance, private
output paths and aggregate audit privacy. API contracts were checked against Riot's
[Match-v5 reference](https://developer.riotgames.com/apis#match-v5),
[League-v4 reference](https://developer.riotgames.com/apis#league-v4), and
[portal documentation](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/portal).
