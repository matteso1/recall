# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Beginner League of Legends players, including the owner, learning itemization and strategic tradeoffs while playing on Windows. This is a general League tool for every champion and role; Xayah is the owner's main and an existing regression fixture, not a product boundary. Users have very little attention to spare during a fight and more time at the shop or after the match.

## Product Purpose

Prepare a loadout, recommend the next useful purchase from actual inventory and available gold, and teach the reusable idea behind the choice. Success means a legal, understandable recommendation; useful adaptation; and a player who increasingly understands the decisions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, encouraging. Explain unfamiliar terms in plain language. Never shame a player for their score, pretend certainty, or issue combat orders.

## Anti-references

The owner rejected the earlier generic companion-app appearance. Preserve the existing compact navy, gold, and teal visual language. Avoid advertising, busy dashboards, stacks of alerts, and long advice while the player is fighting.

## Design Principles

1. The next purchase and its remaining price are the primary information.
2. Make one clear, best-supported recommendation the player can follow immediately. Learning comes through repeated action and a short reason; deeper explanations and alternatives stay optional.
3. Respect actual purchases, manual choices, and the player's preferred direction.
4. Explain uncertainty and unavailable data honestly.
5. Keep recommendations grounded in visible state and patch-specific mechanics.
6. Champion-specific loadouts come from that champion's data; shared algorithms must serve damage dealers, tanks, supports, and junglers without leaking Xayah assumptions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable text at the compact panel size; keyboard-operable controls; visible focus; state labels in addition to color; reduced-motion support. Instructions use plain language with short definitions for itemization concepts.
